How it works

One agent, three minds, hard gates in between.

Each agent has one job. The rules that decide whether anything gets sent live in code, not in a prompt the model could talk its way around.

01

Researcher

Turns a messy job post into structured requirements: skills, seniority, must-haves and keywords.

02

Tailor

Rewrites your resume and cover note using only lines you already have, and cites the source line for every one.

03

Executor

Checks every receipt in code, then uses tool calling to pick which apps to act in. It can do less, never skip a gate.

Apps that work together

Not four integrations. One hand-off.

Each app passes what it made to the next, so you end up with one connected application, not four scattered side effects.

Gmail

Drafts the email with your tailored resume attached. Sends only when you allow it.

Calendar

Books a follow-up in 7 days, with a link straight to that email.

HubSpot CRM

Creates a deal linked to the employer and the candidate, carrying both links. The pipeline lives where teams already work.

Slack

One message with all three links, or why it stopped and what you're missing.

And when they reply: Gmail spots it, the CRM deal moves to Replied, the follow-up reminder cancels itself, and Slack pings you with the link.

Receipts

Every tailored line links to the line of your resume it came from. No source, no send.

Hard gates

Low overlap, an unbacked claim or a repeat application stops the Executor before any app is touched.

Chaos panel

Switch off Gmail, HubSpot, Slack or the model mid-demo. Retries, fallbacks and clear errors, no crashes.

One-click undo

Deletes the draft and the reminder, closes the CRM deal, and tells Slack. Across every app at once.

What it won't do

Boundaries, stated plainly.

  • Invent a skill, employer, number or title you don't have.
  • Send anything without your Google account, your switch and a clean check.
  • Apply to the same role twice.
  • Fail silently. Every outcome reaches you in Slack.
